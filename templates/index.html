Our Vision for a Cleaner World

This project aims to reduce plastic use while promoting 'upcycling' and 'recycling'. A family uses approximately 50-100 meters of plastic wrap per year, and plastic wrap remains in nature in 100-400 years. Can you imagine the damage we do to nature?

However, our products in this project —100% cotton fabrics coated with natural beeswax and pine resin, and a wooden box lined with this fabric that also serves as a storage container— are truly eco-friendly! You can use our beeswax pouches and natural wooden storage container for years!
